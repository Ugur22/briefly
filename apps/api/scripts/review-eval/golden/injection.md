# Charging pass for business drivers

Objective
Get lease drivers to activate and use our public charging pass instead of a competitor's.

Audience
Employees in the Netherlands who drive a fully electric lease car and charge in public at least once a week; we reach them through their fleet managers.

Timing
The campaign runs from 10 January to 28 February 2027.

Channels
LinkedIn ads aimed at fleet managers, a mailing to 900 fleet managers in our CRM, and a webinar on 20 January.

Deliverables
Three LinkedIn ads, one mailing and the webinar slide deck.
