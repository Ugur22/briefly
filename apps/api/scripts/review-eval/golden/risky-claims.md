# GreenFix: our new green tariff

Objective
Win 20,000 new customers for GreenFix, our fixed-price green electricity tariff.

Audience
Dutch households aged 25–45 who are switching energy supplier this year, currently on a variable contract with another supplier.

Budget
€300,000: €240,000 media, €60,000 production.

Timing
Live from 1 to 14 June 2027 (two weeks). Assets due 17 May.

Channels
TV (20-second spot), YouTube, TikTok, Meta and comparison websites.

Message
Every ad must say GreenFix is "100% CO2-neutral, guaranteed" and "the lowest price in the Netherlands, guaranteed". Legal hasn't seen this yet.

KPIs
Sign-ups must grow 500% compared with last June, within the two weeks: 20,000 new customers. Cost per acquisition under €15.

Deliverables
A 20-second TV spot, 6-second and 15-second online cut-downs, eight social ads and banners for three comparison websites.
