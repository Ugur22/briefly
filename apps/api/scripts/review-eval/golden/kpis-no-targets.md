# Heat pump awareness

Objective
Get homeowners to request a free heat pump home scan so we can sell hybrid heat pumps through our installer network.

Audience
Homeowners in the Netherlands with a gas boiler older than 10 years, aged 40–65, in houses built between 1970 and 2000.

Budget
€90,000: €65,000 media, €25,000 production.

Timing
Live from 4 October to 28 November 2027, ahead of the heating season. Assets final on 20 September.

Channels
YouTube, Facebook, regional radio in Noord-Brabant and Limburg, and a banner in our customer app.

KPIs
We want to drive awareness and engagement.

Deliverables
A 20-second radio spot, a 15-second video with two cut-downs, three static social ads and one in-app banner.
