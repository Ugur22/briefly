# VoltHome launch: home charger campaign

Background
VoltHome is our new 11 kW smart home charger, on sale in the Netherlands from 2 March 2027.

Objective
Sell 4,000 VoltHome chargers in the first three months after launch.

Audience
Dutch homeowners aged 30–55 who bought or leased a fully electric car in the last 12 months, or have one on order, and have a driveway or private parking space.

Budget
€250,000 in total: €180,000 working media and €70,000 for production and agency fees.

Timing
Assets final by 9 February 2027. The campaign runs from 2 March to 31 May 2027.

Channels
Meta and YouTube paid social, Google Search, a dealer point-of-sale kit for 120 showrooms, and an email to our 60,000 existing energy customers who drive an EV.

KPIs
4,000 chargers sold by 31 May; 2.5% click-to-quote rate on paid social; cost per sale under €45.

Deliverables
One 30-second and three 6-second videos, six static social ads, search ad copy, a landing page, the dealer kit (poster, counter card, leaflet) and one customer email.
