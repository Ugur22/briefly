# Solar subscription launch

Overview
We launch SunShare, a monthly solar panel subscription with no upfront cost, on 1 March 2027.

Objective
Sign up 1,500 SunShare customers in the first quarter after launch.

Audience
Owners of single-family homes in Utrecht and Gelderland with a south- or west-facing roof who don't have solar panels yet.

Budget
€120,000 total, of which €90,000 is media and €30,000 production.

Timeline
Launch date: 15 March 2027. All ads go live on launch day and run for eight weeks. Final assets are due two weeks before launch.

Channels
Facebook and Instagram ads, Google Search, and a door-to-door leaflet in 40,000 households.

KPIs
1,500 sign-ups by the end of June; cost per sign-up below €80; 3% landing page conversion.

Deliverables
Four social ads, search ad copy, an A5 leaflet and a landing page.
